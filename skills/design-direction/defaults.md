# Defaults

Looks a generator, a starter kit or a theme reaches for when nothing points elsewhere. People
have seen them thousands of times; one is enough to make a screen read as "made by AI" or "made
from a template". A match is a decision to make on purpose, never by default: change it, or
record why this product keeps it.

## Colour

- warm cream ground with a terracotta or rust accent, often with a serif
- near-black ground with one acid green, vermilion or electric blue accent
- a purple-to-blue or pink-to-orange gradient: hero, button, text or border
- violet or indigo as the brand colour with no reason from the product
- a soft glow or blurred colour orb behind a dark hero or card
- grey interface with a single brand-blue accent and nothing else
- the component library's stock palette unchanged (slate or zinc greys, `blue-600`, `indigo-500`)
- pure neutral greys with no tint toward the brand hue
- success green, warning amber and danger red sprinkled as decoration where no status exists
- a reference product's palette taken over as-is ("like GitHub" arriving as GitHub's own greens,
  reds and greys) instead of the product's own brand colours

## Type

- one geometric or neo-grotesque sans for everything (Inter, Geist, the system stack), with no
  second voice
- a thin high-contrast serif display with an italic accent word
- monospace labels used as decoration rather than for data
- gradient or outlined headline text
- a heading scale that steps evenly (each level a little bigger) so nothing leads

## Shape and surface

- the same large radius (`rounded-xl`, `rounded-2xl`) on every block, button and input
- every section in a white card with a 1px light border and a soft shadow
- cards inside cards; the same radius and shadow on every block
- an accent bar on the side of rounded cards
- glassmorphism, frosted panels or noise texture with no tie to the subject
- pill buttons and pill badges everywhere

## Layout — pages

- everything centred; a hero sized to the viewport
- a pill badge above the headline ("✨ New: …")
- a small tracked-caps label above every heading
- three feature cards in a row, each with an icon in a tinted square on top
- a bento grid of feature tiles
- a row of big stats ("10k+ users · 99.9% uptime")
- a logo wall, then a testimonial grid with round avatars and five stars
- three pricing tiers with the middle one highlighted
- numbered markers (01 / 02 / 03) on content that is not a sequence
- a sign-up box closing every page

## Layout — application screens

- a dashboard that opens on four metric cards, each with a green "+12.5%" and a sparkline
- a sidebar of icon + label entries, a search field and an avatar menu top right, and nothing
  that says which product this is
- lists as card grids where a table would let people compare
- a badge on every row, an icon on every button, a tooltip on every control
- a chart because the space was free, with data nobody asked for
- an empty state with a large illustration and "No items yet"
- every edit in a modal
- the welcome banner "Welcome back, Alex 👋"
- toasts that say "Success!"
- AI features marked with a sparkle icon (✨) and a purple gradient

## Imagery and devices

- emoji or generic icons as section markers
- stock photography of people pointing at screens
- abstract blobs, mesh gradients, waves or dot grids with no tie to the subject
- flat vector illustrations of people with oversized limbs in rounded frames
- 3D-rendered glossy shapes or isometric illustrations
- a fake browser or phone frame around a screenshot as the main hero image

## Copy

- one coloured or gradient word in a headline
- a headline built as "X, but Y" or "The Z for W"
- "seamless", "effortless", "powerful", "unlock", "supercharge", "elevate", "streamline",
  "revolutionize", "next-generation", "built for the modern …"
- three-part lists in every sentence, and an em-dash in every other one
- "Get started" as the only call to action
- placeholder people and companies (John Doe, Jane Smith, Acme Inc) and round numbers
- exclamation marks, "Oops!", "Whoops!", "Successfully …"

## Motion

- every block fading and sliding up as it scrolls into view
- hover lifts with a growing shadow on every card
- a counter animating up to each number
